# AP Course & Exam Description (CED) Catalog — 2026-27

A reference list of every AP course and its official College Board **Course and Exam Description (CED)**, gathered in September 2026 as groundwork for a future project.

> **How this was compiled.** The research environment could not reach `collegeboard.org` directly, so it was built from web-search results pointing at the official AP Central PDFs and course pages. CED links marked ✅ showed up in search at that exact URL. Links marked 🔗 follow AP Central's standard naming pattern or point to the course page, which links to the CED. Unit counts and exam formats for courses that did **not** change in 2026-27 come from the current CEDs and should be checked against the PDFs before anything is built on them.

All CED PDFs live under `https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/…`, and all course pages under `https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/<slug>`.

## What changed for 2026-27 (first affected exams: May 2027)

| Change | Courses | Key details |
|---|---|---|
| **New courses** (AP Career Kickstart) | AP Business with Personal Finance, AP Cybersecurity | Yearlong, no prerequisites, fully digital, industry-endorsed credential. First exams: Business on Tue May 4 2027, Cyber on Wed May 5 2027. |
| **Statistics revision** | AP Statistics | 8 units cut to **5**. Algebra II prerequisite removed. Exam goes **fully digital**: **42 MCQ with 4 choices** (was 40 with 5) and **4 FRQ worth 10 pts each** (was 6, and the Investigative Task is gone). Topics removed: departures from linearity, combining random variables, geometric distribution, chi-square goodness of fit, and inference for slopes (old Unit 9). Topics added on investigative questions. |
| **History exam update** | APUSH, AP World: Modern, AP European | MCQ unchanged. **SAQ: all 3 required, no choice** (Q1 secondary source, Q2 primary source, Q3 non-text source) and they cover wider periods. **LEQ: one required, broad prompt**, no choice among questions. DBQ covers a wider chronological range. Two updated practice exams released in 2026-27. |
| **World language revision** | Chinese, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Spanish Language & Culture | Shared skills and exam design across all six languages. Cultural presentation replaced by a **course project** (presentation plus Q&A) on a topic released months ahead. For 2026-27 the theme is Unit 3: Art & Creativity, with music as the lens. A **Personalized Project Reference (PPR)** is due Apr 30 via AP Digital Portfolio. European languages: **55 MCQ in 80 min** (was 65 in 95). Fully digital. Sources disagree on the project's weight (20% vs 35%), so check the CED. |
| **Exam mode changes** | World languages, Spanish Lit, Statistics → fully digital. Music Theory → hybrid digital | By 2027 every AP exam runs in Bluebook. *Fully digital:* everything answered in Bluebook. *Hybrid:* questions shown in Bluebook, FRQ answers handwritten in paper booklets. |
| **Revised CED** | AP Microeconomics | CED is now labeled "Effective Fall 2026". Details of the changes weren't in the search results, so check the PDF. |

Official change pages: [AP Course and Exam Changes](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/how-ap-develops-courses-and-exams/course-changes-overview) · [AP History Exam Updates](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-history-exam-updates) · [AP Statistics Revisions](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-statistics/future-revisions) · [World Language Revisions](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-world-languages-revisions) · [Exam modes by course](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/exam-administration-ordering-scores/administering-exams/digital-ap-exams/exam-modes)
