New music from old motifs.

MotifForge seeds a random melody, breaks it into short motifs, then transposes, reverses, and inverts those motifs to weave them into a brand-new piece — the same trick composers have used for centuries, automated in your browser.

How this works

A random walk through the chosen scale produces a 4-bar seed melody. That seed is split into motifs of 3–4 notes each, labeled A, B, C…. To build the new piece, every motif is introduced once in its original form, then revisited later — sometimes transposed (shifted up or down in pitch), sometimes played in retrograde (backwards), sometimes inverted (its melodic shape flipped upside-down). That mix of repetition and variation is what makes the result sound composed rather than random.
