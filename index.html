⚡ StudyVibe
Built by the Vibe Coding Club

Flashcards that actually stick.

StudyVibe schedules your reviews with spaced repetition so you spend time on what you're about to forget — not what you already know. Free, no sign-up, works offline, and every deck can be shared with a single file.

🧠

Spaced repetition

Every card gets its own schedule. Nail it and the interval grows; miss it and it comes right back.

📤

Share a deck in one click

Export any deck to a small JSON file and text it to a classmate. They import it and they're studying in seconds.

🔥

Streaks & stats

Track your daily streak and cards reviewed so studying feels a little bit like a game.

⏱️

Built-in focus timer

A Pomodoro-style timer for study sessions — because flashcards are better with breaks.

🔒

100% local & private

Everything lives in your browser's storage. No account, no server, no tracking.

🛠️

Open source

It's a club project — read the code, fork it, or add a feature for the next meeting.
